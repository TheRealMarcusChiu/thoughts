# Example

A friend steals from you. (2 things happen or actually 2 things are broken: a law is broken and a relationship is broken) Justice and mercy focuses on reconciling the breaking of the law. While forgiveness focuses on reconciling the relational debt/brokenness (between the offender and offended).

-   **Mercy** - "You deserve punishment, but I will not report you." The focus is on withholding a deserved-consequence (i.e. justice) for breaking the law
-   **Forgiveness** - "I release my anger and my claim against you. I will not define you by this offense. (I no longer see you as one who steals, I see you as a friend)". The focus is on removing the relational debt.

You can have one without the other:

-   **Mercy Without Forgiveness** - a judge may reduce a criminal's sentence but still believe the person is guilty and untrustworthy
-   **Forgiveness Without Mercy** - a victim may forgive someone personally but still believe legal consequences are necessary
